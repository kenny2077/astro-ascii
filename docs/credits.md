*The Quiet Predator* is by Ali Alobaidly. Its reference photograph, generated restyles, and source-embedded local page remain outside this public repository. The same exclusion applies to the photo-derived Walensee study until source rights are documented. A software license does not grant rights to those images.

New photography contributions must identify the original creator, source URL, license or written permission, and required attribution. Merely being available to download does not establish redistribution permission.

Solar-system visual references, consulted 2026-10-11: [NASA planet overview](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/), [Uranus facts](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/facts/), and [Neptune](https://science.nasa.gov/neptune/). These inform subject selection and broad visual traits; the artwork is not a scientific reconstruction. Solar color is an artistic gold treatment, Earth geography is approximate, and display rotations are not physical periods.
