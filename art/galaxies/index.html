Galaxies

Four spirals, modelled and drawn in dots.

Astro ASCII

Andromeda

Each galaxy is a procedural model built from its real shape: tilt, arm pitch, bulge, dust and star-forming regions. Nothing is traced from a photograph. They are styled after these photos: Triangulum by Chuhong Yu, Chunsheng Xie and Zuoming Wang, Sculptor by Mark McComiskey, and Andromeda by Chuhong Yu (all ZWO Astronomy Photographer of the Year 2026). The Pinwheel photo's credit is pending. The motion is sped up and drawn for effect. It is not a simulation.
