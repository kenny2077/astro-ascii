# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Galaxies: Triangulum, Sculptor, Andromeda and the Pinwheel. They are procedural spirals whose discs turn, with orbiting stars and a motion each of their own. The galaxies are now the preview site's first piece. Their colours are muted and their light a little dimmer, and Andromeda is shown whole.

### Fixed

- The black-hole caption no longer covers the artwork, and the black hole fills a phone's width.
- On phones, the preview site's piece tabs stay on one scrollable row.
- The photo study's interface is now in English, like the rest of the collection. Its empty stage now offers a photo picker.
- The GIF exporter's usage notes name the right script path.

### Changed

- Enabled animation for all nine solar portraits; strengthened cloud motion and added accelerated illumination phases over fixed rocky terrain.

- Added a GitHub Pages preview with solar-system, black-hole, and local-photo navigation.
- Removed glowing particle effects; motion now stays within the existing atmospheric or solar material.

- Fixed every solar-system viewing angle and dot layout; replaced rotation with slow, localized light changes.

- Replaced the solar system's synthetic terrain with credited photo-derived maps.
- Removed the solar viewer's strict-ASCII option; retained a single dot style.
- Removed the clipped RGB exposure boost, enlarged the globes, and increased dot resolution.

### Added

- Solar-system collection: Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, now with a 12-second material cycle and illumination-phase animation for Mercury, Mars and Uranus.

- Procedural black-hole artwork and an existing animated GIF preview.
- Local-photo ASCII / pixel comparison with no bundled third-party photograph.
- Source checks, frame tests, CI, contribution guide, and attribution records.

This is the initial collection, not a stable SDK release.
